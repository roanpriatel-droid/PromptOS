The method

Why these actually work.

i
One job per prompt.

A prompt that tries to do four things does none of them. Every Promptos prompt has a single, specific job — and asks the model for the exact shape of output you need.

ii
Tested before it ships.

Every prompt is run against Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini. If it doesn't survive at least 20 real uses, it doesn't get a number.

iii
Specific over clever.

The best prompts read like a brief, not like a magic spell. We write them the way a senior person would write a request to a junior person — clear, opinionated, and concrete.

iv
Editable on purpose.

Ship as .docx so you can fork it, brand it, tune it. The version on your machine should be different from the version on someone else's.

The Hook Ladder
MKT-02.04 · tested against Claude 3.5, GPT-4o, Gemini 1.5
Job
Generate ten ad hooks for one product, ranked from safest to sharpest, so you can pick the angle that fits your channel.
When to use
New campaign, new audience, or when an existing creative is fatigued and you need fresh angles fast.
Tuning notes
Replace [PRODUCT] with one sentence — what it is, who it's for, what pain it removes. Don't paste your whole landing page. Hooks degrade past 20 words; if Claude gives you longer ones, ask for ≤15.
The prompt
You are a paid social copywriter who has shipped 200+ ads for D2C and SaaS. Product: [ONE SENTENCE — what it is, who it's for, what pain it removes] Channel: [META | TIKTOK | LINKEDIN | YT SHORTS] Voice constraint: [e.g. "no hype, no exclamation points, no questions"] Give me 10 ad hooks, each ≤15 words, ordered from "safest, would-pass-legal" to "sharpest, would-make-the-CMO-nervous". For each, add one line of why this hook would land for this channel. Do not number with #1 best — these are options, not a ranking. Skip greetings.
Common variants
Swap "ad hooks" for "subject lines", "email PS lines", or "video cold opens". Add an angle constraint ("only objection-led hooks") for narrower output.

About

We sell prompts. That's it.

No course. No subscription. No "AI agency". One thing — packs of prompts that actually work — done well, with no plans to stretch beyond that.

I started Promptos because I was tired of paying for prompt packs that read like Mad Libs and reading "10 ChatGPT prompts to 10x your productivity" listicles that all linked to the same Notion template.

I'd been writing prompts in a private Google Doc for a year. Friends would ask "how did you get Claude to do that?" and I'd paste three lines into a DM. After enough DMs, I sorted my Doc into seven packs by audience, tested every prompt against three models, and rewrote the ones that didn't survive twenty real uses.

The bar is simple: would you actually keep this prompt in your day-to-day toolkit, or would you forget about it by the end of the week?

The method.

Every prompt in a Promptos pack passes the same four-step check.

One. A real job — pulled from a brief, an investor update, an ad review. Not invented.

Two. Twenty real uses across Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini. If it fails on any model, it gets a tuning note or it gets cut.

Three. Plain language. We don't use hype. The prompt reads like a senior person briefing a junior — clear, opinionated, concrete.

Four. Editable on purpose. Ships as .docx so you can fork it. The version on your machine should be different from the version on someone else's.
